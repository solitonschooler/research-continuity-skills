### What `90_PROVENANCE` means

It contains research claims' source references, scope, version/lineage, proof audits, counterexamples, residuals and uncertainties. Alone it is **not** the theoretical Kernel. The K0, K1, K2, Generative Moves, Results Atlas and provenance must be studied together as needed.

### Version honesty

- `v8.6-light/`: selected, real files from full v8.6, *not* an official independent validated minimal Kernel nor the complete FULL archive.
- `v6.0-provenance-example/`: genuine `CURRENT_V6` inherited inside the v8.6 archive, **not** a complete frozen v6.0 publication (its complete 14-file runtime is not reconstructed here).
- `v4.4-history-excerpt/`: named v4.4-era audit records inherited in v8.6, **not** a complete v4.4 snapshot.
- The original 325 documents are already available in `data/smft/src/`; their stable IDs are in `data/smft/SOURCES_MANIFEST.csv`.

### Scientific and assessment limits

The supplied records include speculative research, local mathematical audits, synthetic experiments and unresolved objections. They do not establish independent scientific validation, trained-Transformer origin, or externally verified reconstruction efficacy. **Do not use these public specimens or published answers as a sealed blind-test set.** We intentionally omit the full `06_BENCHMARK_LOCAL_ONLY`, trial graders, holdouts, and large experiment logs.

### Update rule

These are historical reference snapshots: do not silently rewrite a v6.0 or v4.4 finding to match later revisions. New corrections belong in new addenda with their own provenance.
