# 神之一手 | Generative-Move Ledger v0.3 (stable filename retained)

## 0. Basic principle

A **神之一手** is not simply an “important source”. It is a **non-obvious change of representation, type, ontology, admissibility, or causal question** that unlocks downstream theory. **Origin paper** and **mature demonstration paper** may differ. When a mature paper replaces the immature origin, do not erase the source genealogy.

**A GM is preserved even if its strongest conjecture later fails**, provided the move changes what can be formalized or tested. All GM descriptions below explicitly distinguish **textually source-anchored** from **compiler interpretation**. `M` levels follow `01_KERNEL_COMPILER_SPEC.md`.

## 1. Source-anchored moves (first pilot)

### GM-001 — 觀點先於世界 / Perspective precedes model

- **Origin:** `SRC-0323` §§4a–b, 7.3a (2024 source document; not proof of conceptual inception date).
- **Old frame:** identify a system, then measure its interactions as if its boundary were natural and fixed.
- **Impasse:** the choice of “system”, `天地`, `清濁`, scale and relevance changes what appears to be flowing.
- **Move:** first select a **觀點 / developing subject / 小宇宙**, then identify what constitutes source, sink, flow and causal layers; only afterward model generation and control.
- **Mature refinement:** `SRC-0106` §3 protocol `P`; `SRC-0283` operational frame `Γ_min=(B,Π)`; `SRC-0299` declaration `D:Σ→W_D`.
- **Enables:** causal questions about framing; behavioural quotient; measurement invariance; protocol-conditional cross-domain mapping.
- **Non-entailment:** the mother's viewpoint is not automatically the formal quotient `Π` nor proof of a unique objective partition.
- **Open residual:** object individuation, historical viewpoint shifts and what counts as *the same* object across reframing.
- **Evidence/status:** source-anchored historical comparison, `M1`; future K1 required.

### GM-002 — 「道」由靜態名稱轉為可被接通的流 / A usable difference becomes a maintained flow

- **Origin:** `SRC-0323` §§4b, 5.3d, 7.3, Ch.1.
- **Old frame:** resource and demand treated as independent stocks and a successful transaction as a final outcome.
- **Move:** “道” in the chosen perspective is investigated as the mechanism connecting otherwise disconnected reservoirs/opportunities, such that **qualified flow** is realized and may create conditions for recurrence.
- **Formal anchor:** original concrete P8D (`SRC-0105`) throughput model, P8D II (`SRC-0106`) §§1–2, 4.2, 6–7.
- **Enables:** shared mediator, reinvestment, closure-rank/return-operator questions instead of only stock equilibrium.
- **Non-entailment:** thermodynamic “useful work” or chemical free energy is not automatically equal to economic transaction volume or any semantic-flow scalar.
- **Residual:** specify comparable measurement and units for each domain.
- **Evidence/status:** mother verified; late original P8D needs full K2 audit; `M1/M2`.

### GM-003 — 四對不是八個同型變量 / Typed heterogeneity of four dyads

- **Origin:** `SRC-0323` §5.3b–e, §4.6b; 2024 translation `SRC-0309` Incubation section.
- **Impasse:** traditional octet mappings can look arbitrary; a naive eight-state vector loses the different mathematical kinds of constraints, events and paths.
- **Move:** model **relational questions** as four dyads; allow an *asymmetric type system* and give Kan–Li higher-order role over allowed transformations.
- **Mature anchor:** `SRC-0106` §§2,4–5.
- **Enables:** systematic type checking, falsifiable architecture for adaptive systems, institutional topology changes rather than merely coefficient tuning.
- **Non-entailment:** eight trigrams do not prove an eight-dimensional physical space or a unique decomposition.
- **Residual:** minimality of exactly four dyads remains conjectural (`SRC-0106` §14.5).
- **Evidence/status:** `M2`, model/theory conditional.

### GM-004 — 水火(坎離)由政策「配對」升格為路徑可實現性與制度認可

- **Origin:** `SRC-0323` §5.3e, §4.6, governance discussions Ch.5; mature `SRC-0106` §5.
- **Old frame:** Kan–Li operationally reduced to scalar Memory / Focus or policy–counterpolicy variables.
- **Impasse:** a valid recognized transaction and a physically possible movement are not the same mathematical object; reaching a goal may require multi-step bending around rules.
- **Move:** `Li = ℒ_t(x,a,x')` recognition/admissibility grammar; `Kan = 𝒦_t(goal,x,ℒ,trace,residual)→candidate paths`. Institution evolves with realized paths and trace.
- **Enables:** formal policy/contract/interface constraints; path substitution; route changes; topology-dependent feedback and possible closure-rank change.
- **Non-entailment:** Kan–Li ≠ commitment gate, Li ≠ Gen, and possibility ≠ recognized settlement.
- **Residual:** quantify endogenous institutional change without conflating law, norms and physical safety.
- **Evidence/status:** mature text verified (`M2`); hypotheses about actual domains separately graded.

### GM-005 — 生的順序 vs 以未來為目標的逆向調控

- **Origin:** `SRC-0323` §§4.5–4.6.
- **Old frame:** take classical trigram order only as a temporal chronology or divination sequence.
- **Move:** distinguish (i) *proposed developmental dependency* and (ii) *control planning backwards from desired final state*, in which expectations alter present action and thereby future realization.
- **Enables:** planning/control kernel; time-directed intervention; explicit separation of anticipated future, realized future and target regime.
- **Non-entailment:** no evidence of backwards physical causality; the early text explicitly asks for a concrete causal system before adjudicating the claim.
- **Residual:** strong historical/contextual role of 後天八卦 and precise model for external phase cycle.
- **Evidence/status:** verified origin, `M1` formal adaptation pending.

### GM-006 — 先天 Incubation vs 後天 Adaptation is a product of *two distinct roles*

- **Origin:** `SRC-0309` Ch.1, explicit `Adaptation Trigrams` / `Incubation Trigrams` and `8×8=64` combinations.
- **Impasse:** same trigram names can unintentionally merge incompatible semantics.
- **Move:** treat lower/internal business nature and upper/external macroeconomic phase as **independent classification questions** whose pairing can be tested.
- **Enables:** conditional policy analysis of identical organization under different external regimes; designing phase-dependent interventions.
- **Non-entailment:** combinatoric 64 ≠ exhaustive empirically privileged state space; no guaranteed period of a real macro-cycle.
- **Residual:** evidence for phase inference, transition law, predictive incremental value.
- **Evidence/status:** source verified, `M1` (illustrative economic framework).

### GM-007 — 根苗花果: 孕育不是被動穩態；創新必須買下失敗的權利

- **Origin:** `SRC-0323` §§7.3a–f.
- **Old frame:** growth treated as increasing current throughput, a stable equilibrium or successful replication.
- **Move:** separate viable productive body (根/苗) from the **costly exploratory niche** (花), and from future replication (果). A system may need to sacrifice immediate efficiency to create a viable adaptive basin.
- **Mature anchor:** `SRC-0106` §11 incubation viability, qualified flow, recoverability, residual bound and admissible realization.
- **Enables:** optionality/learning costs, bounded-risk innovation, incubation robustness, agent exploration protocols.
- **Non-entailment:** a calculated basin alone does not quantify innovative option value, and recovery is not identical to evolution.
- **Residual:** explicit branching, experimentation cost, path-dependent learning.
- **Evidence/status:** source-anchored, `M1/M2` pending more formal K1.

### GM-008 — 禮與樂：把協同表象推回依賴的慣性；依賴可反過來生成制度

- **Origin:** `SRC-0323` §5.3f and §999c–d; the source explicitly proposes that 樂 is better characterized as 「依賴的慣性」 than merely “協同”.
- **Old frame:** external rules produce coordination; coordination appears only as a benefit/epiphenomenon.
- **Move:** shared signalling, memory and repeated interaction create *structural dependency*, which can reinforce or distort formal rituals/rules (以禮制樂 / 以樂制禮).
- **Enables:** institutional recursion, stigmergy, social-memory governance, path-dependent social attractors and their negative cases.
- **Non-entailment:** strong persistence does **not** imply beneficial rule, truth, morality, or an identified Transformer circuit.
- **Residual:** formal dependency-inertia measure; coercion/welfare constraints; experimental counterfactuals.
- **Evidence/status:** text verified; mathematical translation `M1`.

### GM-009 — 從高維 state count 轉為低數目的 recurrent closure channels

- **Formal source:** `SRC-0106` §§6–9, 14 (earlier germ may occur in `SRC-0105`, not yet genealogically audited).
- **Move:** analyze `F=F0+Γ∘H`, `H:X→R^r`; ask how many **shared recurrent mediators** are needed rather than how many total coordinates exist.
- **Strict result:** under differentiability, `rank(DF−DF0)≤r`; determinant lemma yields reduced return-operator condition for `J=J0+UV^T`.
- **Enables:** diagnostic of feedback organization without asserting a two-dimensional world; separates mechanism from complex/split spectral signatures.
- **Non-entailment:** no assertion that every interesting real system admits robust low closure rank; small rank without small fitting residual can be misleading.
- **Evidence/status:** conditional mathematical construction/proposition `M3`; empirical generality open.

### GM-010 — 把成界的 quotient 放在命名/本體之前

- **Formal source:** `SRC-0283` abstract and §§1–2, and `SRC-0299` early definitions. Genesis precursor `SRC-0323` §4.
- **Move:** define frame `Γ=(B,Π)` and task-relative behavioral equivalence before claiming object identity. Let multiple operationally adequate quotients coexist.
- **Enables:** separating observer limitation from ontology, exact reduction vs approximate practical models, principled reframing via changed distinctions.
- **Non-entailment:** the coarsest exact quotient under a fixed rich probe protocol is not a claim that finite observers identify a unique world.
- **Residual:** naming/individuation and long-memory compositions, as flagged in Framing source.
- **Evidence/status:** source verified construction, `M2/M3` within assumptions.

### GM-011 — Functional abstraction is not natural semantic individuality

- **Formal source:** `SRC-0261` 2026-10-12 report, abstract and audit.
- **Old frame:** successful port-level input/output equivalence interpreted as discovery of a naturally formed internal “semantic object”.
- **Move:** split **engineered qualified interface** from **naturally individuated causal mechanism**; require counterfactual tests and actual real-Transformer evidence for the latter.
- **Enables:** rigorous experiment design where output loss, joint port closure, actual causal organization and attractor presence can disagree.
- **Non-entailment:** real Transformer natural-attractor existence is not proved by the conditional math or synthetic tests.
- **Residual:** controlled real-model circuit discovery / repeated intervention and cross-context persistence tests.
- **Evidence/status:** verified source conclusion; M2–M4 for conditional/synthetic work; not M5 for natural individuality.

### GM-012 — 花的探索成本與果的擴張成本不是同一種投資

- **Historical origin:** `SRC-0323` §7.3b–e, notably ¶1949–1951 and ¶1963–1975.
- **Move:** isolate current self-maintenance `(根,苗)` from **improvement of the mode** `(花)` and **replication of the mode** `(果)`. In particular, 花 risks internal R&D, partner/interface exchange expenditure, *and displacement by failed trials*.
- **Mature interface:** `SRC-0106` §11 on viable *whole-system* incubation, recovered states and qualified flow. **花 ≠ ℐ_P(h)**; experimental option-value and opportunity cost remain additional dimensions.
- **Conditional mathematical repair:** budgeted `i_t/p_t` choice + gross information-value lemma under no-cost/no-change-of-actions assumptions, written in `K1-DEV-001`, are **compiler constructions**, not source formulas.
- **Proof guard:** Basin membership under `∃π` need not imply a nonzero worst-case recovery radius; require quantified perturbation recovery and specify `∃policy∀shock` vs `∀shock∃policy`.
- **Enables:** separate exploration and replication policies; innovation vs dissemination failure; opportunity cost and survivability tests.
- **Residual:** source genealogy of evolutionary metaphor; causal measurement of option value and failure risk in real targets.
- **Evidence:** historical `M0`, correspondence `M1`, conditional constructions `M2/M3`; no empirical universality claim.

### GM-013 — 時皇: future representation is a current causal variable

- **Historical origin:** `SRC-0323` §4.5b/c ¶1081–1123 (including the explicitly uncertain physical-time analogy).
- **Move:** *Present* signal / future expectation reorganizes current alliances, resource allocation and participation; those actions may validate or defeat the expectation. Thus a not-yet-realized world can organize some current behavior **as an anticipated world**, not as future physical causation.
- **Mature context:** `SRC-0299` Purpose, Gate, Trace, Declaration; `SRC-0279` regime/latching; these are downstream possible engineering mappings, not original 2024 equations.
- **Enables:** anticipation-dependent institutional formation, social equilibrium selection, public-commitment dynamics, credibility and signal interventions.
- **Non-entailment:** neither retrocausality nor relativity equivalence, inevitable self-fulfilment, nor positive moral status; false persuasion remains an unresolved origin distinction.
- **Residual:** distinguish a real new 道 / 時皇 from public manipulation / 霸; empirical belief-intervention methods.
- **Evidence:** `M0` original claims + `M2/M3` compiler examples; no `M5` causality proof for a particular domain.

### GM-014 — 「逆數」 becomes distinct forecast + backward reachable-set design

- **Historical origin:** `SRC-0323` §4.6a–c ¶1127–1166, and `GM-005` predecessor.
- **Move:** distinguish `forward explanation`, `forward forecast using past trace`, and `backward *computation* from an intended final objective to currently admissible actions`. Reverse reasoning is not reverse operation of physical time.
- **Mathematical bridge:** `G_t=Pre_{L,t}(G_{t+1})` with explicit reachability proof for deterministic Markov controlled dynamics (compiler `M3`, `K1-TIME-001` §5); validity depends on specified action sets and Li.
- **Enables:** formal design of leverageable Kan–Li intervention, propagation checks, sustained incubation and target-world attainability; honest unreachable-goal tests.
- **Non-entailment:** no mandated `4-dyad` chronological chain, no control when Li prohibits path, no guarantee that a chosen announcement has effect.
- **Residual:** interactions with phase-dependent Adaptation Trigrams, purpose-change vs mere goal optimization, partially observed multi-agent anticipations.
- **Evidence:** historical `M0`, later translation `M1`, compiler mathematical conditional result `M3`.

## 3. Decisions required before upgrading an intake reference to GM

For each R01–R17:

1. Extract its actual claim/axiom/formula (source page + exact labels).
2. Identify whether this text was the **first origin**, **formal repair**, **reduction**, **illustration**, or **critic**.
3. Write a minimal typed *move*, not only a catchy one-line analogy.
4. Enumerate at least one **false but tempting inference** a powerful future LLM may draw.
5. Search earlier source IDs for precursor; revise `origin` separate from `formal_source`.
6. Link to present K0 and mark an explicit `BRIDGE GAP` when direct derivation does not follow.
7. Construct a reconstruction task that title-only language models are unlikely to answer correctly.

**Do not automatically create 17 GM nodes just because 17 papers were nominated.** A paper can contain 0, 1 or many moves; the same move may occur in several papers. This ledger's `GM-001`–`GM-011` are hypotheses of the best decomposition and will be split/merged on closer source audit.
