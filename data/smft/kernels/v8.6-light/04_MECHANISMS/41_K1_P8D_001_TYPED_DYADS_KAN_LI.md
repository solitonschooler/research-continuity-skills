# K1-P8D-001 — 四對先天八卦：Typed Dyads, Kan–Li Transversality, Recursive Closure

> **Micro-kernel**：四對不是八個同質 scalar nor eight independent coordinates. Qian–Kun = differential→qualified transduction; Gen–Dui = endogenous separation↔exchange; Zhen–Xun = event trigger→propagation; Kan–Li = construction of realizable means under an evolving **admissibility grammar**. Kan–Li is *transversal*: it changes which transitions/closure loops the other three can realize. `Li ≠ Gen ≠ Commit`; `Xun routing ≠ Kan goal-oriented means construction`.

## 3. Kan–Li: the essential non-obvious move

From `SRC-0323` §5.3e, paragraphs `1501–1506`: `制度範圍` (institutional scope) and `曲成手段` (adaptive means) **co-define** effective social/economic behavior; realized transactions can reveal actual institutional scope better than formal rules alone.

Mature P8D II represents the relation:

$$
\mathcal L_t(x,a,x')\in\{0,1\}, \qquad
\Pi_{K,t}=\mathcal K_t(g_t,x_t,\mathcal L_t,\tau_t,R_t).
$$

`SRC-0106` eqs `(5.3)` PDF p.20; `(5.10)` PDF p.22; visually checked in source PDF. Effective and formal institution need not agree:

$$
\mathcal L_t^{F}\ne\mathcal L_t^{E},\qquad
\mathcal L_{t+1}^{E}
=\Gamma_L(\mathcal L_t^{F},\Pi_{K,t},y_t,\tau_t,R_t,e_t).
$$

Source `(5.15)–(5.18)`. This **recursion** should not be collapsed to a constant penalty or binary scalar gate. Realized choices, enforcement and precedent can change the *effective admissibility grammar* itself. Conceptually:

`admissibility → candidate means → realization/outcome → revised effective admissibility`.

The transition relation may be treated as a directed graph with valid edges. Removing/adding valid edges through Li can alter reachable states and feedback cycle structure, not just edge weights. **This graph construction is a compiler illustration of P8D II §5.10**, not an already-proven universal physical law.

**Illustrative falsifiable toy case (compiler-added):** Three states `A,B,C` have a circulation `A→B→C→A`. Formal change invalidates `C→A` while other edges remain. The cycle disappears entirely (graph topology), not merely grows slower. Testable claim in real regimes: do changes in admissibility produce lost/created effective feedback paths, above and beyond fitted parameter drift? The *Kan–Li transversality conjecture* is `SRC-0106` §14.6, not theorem.

**Four not-equals:**

```text
observer cut B_P ≠ Gen's ∂X_G;
Gen crossing ≠ Li recognized transition;
Li admission ≠ Gate commitment;
Xun routing a signal ≠ Kan composing means toward a goal.
```

## 4. Runtime, closure and critical mathematical safeguards

For a declared protocol, the paper proposes (schematically):

$$
\dot x=F_{K,L}(x;\mathcal Q,\mathcal G,\mathcal Z,u)+\eta.
$$

Its multi-channel subclass (source `(6.4)` PDF p.27, visually checked):

$$
F_{K,L}(x)=F_{0}^{K,L}(x)
 +\sum_{\ell=1}^{r}c_{\ell}^{K,L}(x)H_{\ell}^{K,L}(x).
$$

where `H_ℓ` are shared mediated flows and `c_ℓ` describe reinjection. **Critical mathematical guard:** a *sum of r state-dependent vector–scalar products does not, by itself, prove* `rank(DF−DF₀)≤r`. Differentiation also introduces `H_ℓ D c_ℓ`, as P8D II explicitly notes in `(6.8)`. A valid bound follows when a suitable **factorization** is actually established:

$$
F=F_0+\Gamma\circ H,\quad H:X\to\mathbb R^r,
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
DF-DF_0=D\Gamma(H)\,DH,
\quad\operatorname{rank}(DF-DF_0)\le r.
$$

This conditional result appears at `SRC-0106` `(6.12)–(6.15)` / `(14.4)–(14.6)` PDF p.71 (visually checked). If `c_ℓ` depend arbitrarily on x, they may introduce independent derivative directions beyond r. Thus *closure channel count*, *rank of actual Jacobian correction*, and *state dimension* must be tested separately.

**Minimal counterexample (compiler-generated):** `x=(x₁,x₂)`, `r=1`, `H(x)=x₁`, `c(x)=(x₁,x₂)^T`, `F₀=0`. Then `F=(x₁²,x₁x₂)` and

$$
DF(1,1)=\begin{pmatrix}2&0\\1&1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad \operatorname{rank}DF(1,1)=2>r.
$$

This is **not a contradiction of the source's factorized theorem**. It shows exactly why the factorization hypothesis is indispensable. Do not blindly apply the `(6.15)` conclusion to arbitrary `(6.4)` systems.

Moreover, even genuine `rank(DF−DF₀)≤r` **does not imply** `dim(effective dynamics)≤r`. Effective-theory reduction requires a separate compiler `C_P:X→Y` and intertwining over declared probes/horizon, as in `SRC-0106` §12:

$$
DC_P(x)F_P(x)\approx F_{eff}(C_P(x)),
\quad C_P\circ\Phi_t^{P8D}\approx\Phi_t^{eff}\circ C_P.
$$

## 5. Related but NOT absorbed: incubation and later adaptation

`SRC-0323` §7.3c/d, DOCX paragraphs `1968–1987`, defines `花` by allocating scarce current resources to **experiment, intermediary exchange and failed attempts**, rather than simply maximising immediate output. P8D II §11 formalizes a more stringent *Incubation Basin*: viability + qualified flow + bounded residual + Kan–Li realizability + recovery; this is a **bridge**, not identity with the original innovation economics.

A rigid institution may preserve viability while destroying productive exploration; a purely permissive institution may permit unbounded residual and fail. This is why `Productive Admissible Freedom` must be evaluated jointly, not equated with maximum freedom or maximum restriction (`SRC-0106` §11.12–11.14).

**Still separate:** 後天 phase transitions, 道德仁霸, 時皇 expectation effects, Purpose, self-referential Observer, semantic field ontology and Ledger. P8D II §15 explicitly says its runtime is a **mesoscopic layer**, not a replacement for those broader mechanisms.
