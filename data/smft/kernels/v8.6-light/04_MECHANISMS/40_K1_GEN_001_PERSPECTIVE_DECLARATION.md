## 2. Minimal typed mechanism

The following is a **compiler's reconstruction** (`M2` formal proposal) assembled from named source formulas, **not** a single equation authored in 2024.

- `Ω`: ambient possibilities or richer state carrier (no metaphysical commitment).
- `s`: developing subject / phenomenon of interest, chosen operationally; may be a trajectory or outcome pattern, not necessarily an individual object.
- `P=(B_P,Δ_P,h_P,U_P^probe)`: bounded research protocol. `B_P` selects target extent; `Δ_P` determines admitted observations/aggregation; `h_P` is horizon; `U_P^probe` permits experimental interrogations. The superscript **probe** excludes system-internal Li admissibility.
- `X_B`: state carrier inside observer-selected boundary.
- `Π:X_B→Z`: quotient / retained-distinction map; two states can be operationally identified if `Π(x)=Π(y)`.
- `Σ_P`: realized protocol-bound observation trace, not the underlying system `X` itself.
- `Φ_t^X`: underlying/richer state transition or flow, if it exists and is modelled.
- `Φ_t^Z`: candidate effective transition / flow on `Z`.

$$
\Gamma_{\min}=(B,\Pi),\qquad \Pi:X_B\to Z,\qquad
x\sim_{\Pi}y\iff\Pi(x)=\Pi(y).
$$

For a proposed **exact deterministic effective dynamics**, a sufficient well-definedness requirement is **fibre consistency** (for all admitted `x,y` and relevant `u,t`):

$$
\Pi(x)=\Pi(y)\ \Longrightarrow\
\Pi(\Phi^X_{t,u}(x))=\Pi(\Phi^X_{t,u}(y)).
$$

Then one may define `Φ^Z_{t,u}(Π(x)):=Π(Φ^X_{t,u}(x))`, giving:

$$
\Pi\circ\Phi^X_{t,u}=\Phi^Z_{t,u}\circ\Pi.
$$

**Compiler derivation:** the above exact factor-map criterion is standard quotient well-definedness, imported to clarify `SRC-0283` / `SRC-0106` §12. It is **not** a claim that the real SMFT system already satisfies it. Approximate/finite-horizon reductions additionally require explicit norm, tolerance, task, horizon and probes.

## 3. Three genuinely different boundaries/gates (never flatten)

| Object | Type / question | Counterexample to confusion |
|---|---|---|
| `B_P` Observer cut | Which organization did the investigator include? | Studying a *bank* is not the same as bank's internal collateral segregation |
| `∂X_G` Gen boundary | Which interface does the system **itself** maintain for separation/exchange? | Funds can cross a departmental boundary without being a legally cleared transaction |
| `ℒ_t(x,a,x')` Li admission | Does transition count as valid/qualified under effective institution? | Technically delivered data can fail schema and be rejected |
| `G_commit` (later) | Which admissible candidate becomes committed history? | Multiple legal orders exist; only one is submitted, settled and recorded |

A changed `P` alters interpretation/trace without logically requiring physical change; conversely the physical organization can change at fixed `P`. This is a **type distinction** rather than a claim that either interpretation is automatically correct.

## 6. Bridging failure and falsifiers

- `BF-GEN-01` **Pseudo-frame:** changing `Π` gives wildly unstable predictive classes under minimal permissible perturbations; protocol cannot support reproducible analysis.
- `BF-GEN-02` **Non-Markov quotient:** `Π(x)=Π(y)` but next projected state differs under the same intervention; no autonomous exact effective dynamics exists on `Z` alone. Fix by adding history or state distinctions—not by pretending exact semiconjugacy.
- `BF-GEN-03` **Boundary confusion:** an observational boundary change is recorded as a regulatory (Li) change, corrupting causal attribution.
- `BF-GEN-04` **False ontological leap:** a stable quotient is declared an intrinsically existing semantic entity or real Transformer circuit without intervention evidence.

**Minimal counterexample for BF-GEN-02 (compiler-generated, not source theorem):** let rich state `(x,y)∈R²`, `Π(x,y)=x`, and transition `(x,y)↦(x+y,y)`. Rich states `(0,1)` and `(0,2)` have the same `Π` but next projected values `1` and `2`; no deterministic one-step `Φ^Z` on `x` alone reproduces both.
