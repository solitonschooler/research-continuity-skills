## B. The preserved mother's conceptual axes

### G1 — 「觀點」是模型存在的先決條件（不是一種模型參數）

**Genesis passage**: `SRC-0323` §4a–4b first asks what small universe is being analysed, how 清/濁 is distinguished under a selected 觀點, what 陰陽大流 passes through it, and on which 天/地/人 layers the interaction operates; only then does the text discuss generators or governance. §7.3a independently says **identify the developing object first, not the environment**.

**Formal refinement**: `SRC-0106` §3 declares an observation protocol, represented there as:

\[
P=(B_P,\Delta_P,h_P,U_P^{\mathrm{probe}}),\qquad
\Sigma_P=\operatorname{Log}(\mathrm{System}\mid P).
\]

`B_P` is investigator-declared boundary; `Δ_P` is aggregation/measurement; `h_P` horizon; `U_P^probe` probe family. Do NOT substitute `Σ_P` for the physical system itself.

`SRC-0283` develops a narrower **framing** kernel:

\[
\Gamma_{\min}=(B,\Pi),\quad \Pi:X_B\to Z,
\quad x\sim_\Pi y\iff\Pi(x)=\Pi(y).
\]

Thus an operational world depends jointly on a chosen boundary and retained distinctions. **Bridge statement (M1/M2):** 2024 選觀點 → later protocol declaration/framing. Not identical: early 清濁/陰陽 may include additional thermodynamic or normative readings not captured by a bare quotient.

**Kernel warning:** `Boundary of observer B_P` ≠ `system-generated exchange boundary Gen` ≠ `institutionally admissible transition Li` ≠ `commitment gate`. P8D II §3.4 distinguishes the first three explicitly.

### G2 — A world needs a drive, a sustaining enclosure and a propagation mechanism

`SRC-0323` §5.3 (especially 5.3b–d):

- **天地定位 / 乾–坤**: a declared relation between distinguishable resource reservoirs, asymmetries and possible productive flow; commercial examples include capacity/demand, investment/market. These are **contextual representations** not universal definitions of Qian/Kun.
- **山澤通氣 / 艮–兌**: a held environment with buffering, retention, exchange and controlled accommodation enabling a cycle to persist; examples credit, spring, wetland or niche; not literally always a buffer stock.
- **雷風相薄 / 震–巽**: trigger and propagation / chain reaction, sometimes reinvested reproduction; an eruption alone is transient if containment and renewed resources are absent.
- **水火（不）相射 / 坎–離**: institutional rules and adaptive, curved realization — not another inventory/flow scalar pair.

`SRC-0106` §§4–5 formalizes these as **typed relational dyads**:

| Dyad | Type/question | Not this |
|---|---|---|
| Qian–Kun | Potential ↔ qualified transduction: what gradient/means turns possible difference into flow? | Fixed “supply and demand” ontology |
| Gen–Dui | Boundary ↔ exchange: what is retained/released across endogenous boundary? | Merely one buffer equation |
| Zhen–Xun | Trigger ↔ propagation: what event activates what relay/spreading path? | Every route counts as successful realization |

**Key upgrade:** Kan–Li operates on a transition grammar and can alter connectivity of the **other** channels. Treat typed asymmetry as a feature rather than shoehorning all eight roles into eight homogeneous variables.

### G3 — 「順生」與「逆控」是不同 direction-of-reasoning

`SRC-0323` §4.6b gives a candidate developmental direction:

`天地定位 (establish flow opportunity) → 山澤通氣 (sustain/incubate) → 風雷相薄 (replicate/expand) → 水火不相射 (co-regulate institutional relation)`.

The **control perspective** reverses the planning/question direction: from a future goal to govern Kan–Li, trigger a propagation chain, sustain a viable buffer/ecology, and ultimately establish a **new** economic “world”. This is not a claim of universally fixed chronological succession; §4.6 itself stresses concrete specification before analysis and includes possible non-necessary transitions.

`時皇` (§4.5) likewise allows future expectation to change present decisions, hence the *realized future*:

\[
\widehat{F}_{t+h}\to u_t\to F_{t+h},
\]

where `widehat F` is a forecast or socially shared expectation, `u_t` intervention, and `F` future state. **Compiler notation**, not original equation; this is ordinary anticipatory feedback, not verified retrocausality.

### G4 — 先天 incubation grammar is not 後天 adaptation clock

### G6 — 道・德・仁・霸 cannot be compressed into the four dyads

## C. Mathematical checkpoint: exactly what later P8D II contributes

### C1. Runtime architecture

`SRC-0106` §6 presents a useful subclass of runtime vector fields:

\[
\dot x=F_{K,L}(x),\qquad
F_{K,L}(x)=F^{K,L}_0(x)+\sum_{\ell=1}^r c^{K,L}_\ell(x)H^{K,L}_\ell(x).
\]

It separates baseline/direct dynamics from a few recurrent mediated channels. The number `r` counts **effective feedback mediators in a declared representation**, not eight trigrams, not state dimension and not always an intrinsic invariant.

### C2. Conditional low-rank result

If a suitable local factorization holds, for example:

\[
F(x)=F_0(x)+\Gamma(H(x)),\quad H:X\to\mathbb R^r,
\]

then `rank(DF−DF0) ≤ r` under differentiability assumptions. `SRC-0106` §14 labels this a mathematical consequence, but whether a real-world system *actually admits a useful small-r factorization* is a **separate empirical conjecture**. Use `closure residual ε_cl` as well as rank to avoid spurious “low rank” success.

### C3. Return operator before complex-plane interpretation

Given a linearization `J = J0 + U V^T`, the resolvent/return operator is `R(ζ)=V^T(ζI−J0)^{-1}U` where defined, and the determinant factorization follows under the same conditions. Only if an empirically justified effective `2×2` sector is found do complex-like / split-like signatures have legitimate status. `SRC-0106` §§8–9: **the complex structure is a result, not a premise**. A resonance analogy is not by itself a complex-valued state equation.

### C4. Realization vs admissibility (distinct from gate)

`SRC-0106` §5 defines Li as `ℒ_t(x,a,x')∈{0,1}` (whether a transition counts as admissible); Kan as a path constructor:

\[
\mathcal K_t:(g_t,x_t,\mathcal L_t,\tau_t,R_t)\to\Pi_{K,t}.
\]

The physical boundary of exchange and the institutional validity of an action differ; these in turn differ from **commitment**, which is part of the broader World-Formation architecture. In particular, a path being possible does not mean its output is committed as a record.

### C5. Effective theory not by slogan

`SRC-0106` §12 requires a declared compiler `C_P` to preserve targeted flow under bounded error and time horizon:

\[
\sup_{0\le t\le h}\left\| C_P(\Phi^{P8D}_t(x))-\Phi^{eff}_t(C_P(x))\right\|
\le\varepsilon_{red}.
\]

For intervention-bearing claims, compare intervention signs and relevant outcomes. “The equations look similar” is not enough.

### C6. Runtime is not the whole World-Formation Theory

`SRC-0106` §15 explicitly gives the interface:

`Declaration → P8D Runtime → Commitment → Trace → Residual → Revision`.

This **division of labor** is crucial: do not expand P8D until it swallows Observer, Purpose, naming, all quantum geometry and ledger. It is a **mesoscopic** layer; broader world-formation includes frame declarations and admissible revision.

### F1. Rich state, declared probes, downstream observable

Define

\[

 k\in\mathbb Z_4,\quad e,o,\ell,n\in\mathbb Z_2,
 \qquad |S|=64.
\]

Interpretation: `k` is a discrete relative-phase label for a notional normalized complex vector `ψ_k=(|0⟩+i^k|1⟩)/√2`; `e` is an environment bit; `o` is observer/selection-memory state; `ℓ` is the previous admitted binary record; `n` is a truly decoupled nuisance coordinate. The actual **emitted, jointly auditable record** is `Y=(y,a)` with `a=e XOR o` (audit/readiness), rather than merely `y`. Output choice is a crucial part of **protocol declaration**.

Controls: `u=0` Z-like phase-insensitive observation, `u=1` X-like phase-sensitive observation, and `u=2` an *unreported mixture* with 1/8 X-like readout weight (a weak probe). \[
\Pr(y=1\mid s,u)=
\begin{cases}
\tfrac12,&u=0,\\
\tfrac{1+\cos(\pi k/2)}2,&u=1,\\
\tfrac12+\tfrac1{16}\cos(\pi k/2),&u=2.
\end{cases}
\]

Conditional on emitted `y` the full state updates as

\[
\begin{aligned}
 k'&=(k+e+u+y)\bmod4,\\
 e'&=e\oplus(o\wedge y),\\
 o'&=o\oplus(e\wedge y)\oplus\ell,\\
 \ell'&=y,\\
 n'&=n\oplus((1-\ell)\wedge y).
\end{aligned}
\tag{F-v09-1}
\]

This makes `e` and `o` alter future evolution, emits a record and allows the record to feed back into both `k` and `o`. `n` changes but never affects the protected coordinates or the observable `Y`, giving a non-trivial exact compression control.
