# 03 — 代表性實例：怎樣保留生成性但防止錯誤推導

These cases are **anchors for skill-design experiments**, not a replacement for the cited sources and K2. Cross-check original versions when building the final Skill.

## Case A — Dual Ledger: a cross-document error needs lineage, not repeated 'discoveries'

- Sources: `SRC-0119` and later `SRC-0229`; W257–259 and W350; L0 `RA-M06`.
- Correct conjugate calculus when `Φ=ψ*` at matching `s,λ`: `dΦ=λ·ds`, `dψ=s·dλ`, `d(Φ+ψ)=d(λ·s)`.
- The source-specific balance `ΔΦ=W_s−Δψ` need not hold. Take `Φ=ψ=s²/2`, `λ=s`, transition `0→1`: `ΔΦ=W_s=Δψ=1/2`; wrong RHS zero.
- Compilation action: retain the two original source assertions, attach **one common proof defect family**, show corrected identity; label later propagation, not an independent mathematical innovation.

## Case B — L0 vs proof: exact quotient depends on task and intervention

- W41/W154, L0 `RA-M01`, PF01. `π:S→P` is exact only relative to chosen actions/readouts when outputs and quotient transitions factor through `π`.
- Do not infer from attractive low-dimensional dashboards that the hidden dynamics can be compressed in every task. This matters for PORE Ξ, Agent memory, attractor ports, and phase clocks.

## Case C — Same final answer does not validate execution trace

- `SRC-0234` Appendix G, W308, PF13, L0 `RA-M02`.
- `π:X→Y` maps full runs to visible outputs, `T` denotes authorized valid runs. Endpoint-only verification iff `T=π⁻¹(π(T))`.
- A valid and invalid trace can share a final answer. This **differs** from exact dynamical quotient and must not be de-duplicated into PF01.

## Case D — A coordinate identity can masquerade as discovery of residual dynamics

- `SRC-0043`, W316, PF14, L0 `RA-M09`.
- For `Z=A exp(iθ)` with `A=|Z|>0` and `θ=arg Z` from the same differentiable `Z`, `dot Z=(dot A/A+i dot θ)Z` is an identity; an endogenous `ε_dyn` therefore equals zero.
- Independent prediction `ĝ,ω̂` with held-out data can define a nonzero empirical residual. The compiler must record this oracle distinction.

## Case E — A 9-cycle theorem is narrower than 'the cosmos naturally has nine states'

- L0 `RA-M03/M04` and K2-NINE-012.
- `T_f(a,b)=(a+1,b+f(a)) mod 3` is a single cycle on nine states iff `Σ_{a=0}^2 f(a)≠0 mod 3`. `AGL(2,3)` has no order-9 member.
- Do not infer the phenomenon for arbitrary two ternary systems or for actual celestial/physical mechanisms. Preserve theorem and cultural generative intuition separately.

## Case F — A typed similarity may be useful engineering without mathematical isomorphism

- SIDA phase/fold/slot mappings, `SRC-0150`, W185/W268, PF09.
- Node-label bijection is not a transition-graph isomorphism; verify edges, state observables and permissible action sequences.
- Correct output: an implementation-ready translation plus an explicit *unproven bridge*, not silently declaring a cross-domain equivalence.

## Case G — Physical terms require physical mathematical contracts

- Belt / Gauge / Spinor: `SRC-0274`, W236–238; `SRC-0240`, W367. A phase coupling `1−cos(θᵢ−θⱼ)` has shared/global phase invariance but not arbitrary independent local gauge transformations unless transforming link connections exist. Two runtime ledger closures do not generate Spinor `4π` periodicity.
- This is a **type boundary**, not a reason to delete the accounting/control engineering idea.

## Case H — Natural vs synthetic functional structures

- `SRC-0259/0260/0261`, W272/W286, L0 `RA-E14/RA-M11`.
- Stable coupled dynamics ≠ sufficient Effective Port ≠ correct operator ≠ naturally learned Transformer function. Each arrow requires different oracle/intervention and perhaps different source.
- Save the mature historical tightening of a conjecture, including non-realization or null results.
