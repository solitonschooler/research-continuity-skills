Actual procedure: record model/probe discrepancy and recover conditional research question: which proxies add prospective ability prediction beyond supervised linear readout and training loss? Test with bigger/harder tasks, truly delayed generalization, frozen predictor, independent heldout splits; no posthoc threshold. Confidence limited (8 test inputs; not independently replicated); never turn null into confirmation.

## Generalizable Skill design requirements and evidence limits

- Require changed-outcome denominator, upstream action propagation, fair rivals, no reused holdout, metric sensitivity/gauge control, multiple seeds, and independent grading when available.
