# v6.5 Skill Field Notes — Reproducible Control Nulls, Causal Repairs, and Creative Residual

**Skill research EXPERIENCE, NOT a runnable GitHub Skill.** Source-scoped (`SRC-0261` p6; `SRC-0130` p23; `SRC-0298` pp8–9; `SRC-0299` p7). Existing 26 Markdown are unchanged by this note. `EXPERIMENTS_V65/` is a new fully reproducible deterministic toy assay, not independent LLM research inference.

## Lessons demonstrably learned this version

1. Converting a theoretical condition into a reproducible experiment requires **exposing the complete equation, fixed parameter grid, action sequence, output CSV and pass/fail criterion**. Run 300 trajectories ×12 steps, report 0 bound violations on the declared system, and *never* call this a verified real semantic attractor.
2. A mathematical theory label may add no information. An ordinary Lipschitz/state-abstraction baseline with the **same λ and defect data** computes *identically* to Attractor-framed contraction. A baseline that discards known λ and uses L=1 is intentionally less informed: an apparent uplift against it cannot be credited to the new theory.
3. Contradiction/repair is granular: `SRC-0130` p23 first-order Gate is valid as a differential diagnostic but not full finite-step safety. 6 of 24 directional-positive choices worsen actual quadratic loss; repair by exact discrete difference, no straw-man rejection of the source's local differential.
4. Second-order correction is *still not enough* for causal intervention. 8 of 75 toy interventions improve before/after while harming relative to no action. Turn this into **productive Residual**: preregister matched no-action arm and only then allow causal-benefit claims. This is a more constructive next research move than another shallow counterexample.
5. Observer decision certificates are **selective**. 1,870/3,600 score margins certified, zero disagreements among certified, but many uncertified decisions correct; coverage, selection bias and sensitivity across operating regimes must be reported separately.
6. Nonautonomous switched contractions yield a forced 2-cycle rather than a unique fixed point. A future Skill must check whether analogies presume autonomous attractors, shared control traces or stationary boundaries before proposing dynamical explanatory links.
