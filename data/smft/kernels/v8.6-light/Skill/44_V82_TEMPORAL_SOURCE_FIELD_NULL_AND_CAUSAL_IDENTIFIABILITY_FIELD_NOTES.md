**Case ID:** V82-HETU-TEMPORAL-01; GL82-01..03; RA-H04; supplemental primary source from v8.1 PDF (SHA in V81 audit). **Evidence:** compiler-authored 80 synthetic trials, exact generator and matching code, heldout CSV, 24 QA checks + inherited 19, NOT a new neural/independent scientific trial.

**Trigger:** v8.1 recovered five *planted* shared-factor pairs, but user explicitly stressed Hetu as prior trained source body with center, stable bifrequency resonances, phase/orientation and recovering tension, before any projected LuoShu. Pairing alone was an incomplete proxy.

**Actual procedure:** Before primary run freeze four conditions FULL, PAIR_ONLY, CENTER_ONLY, NONE, 20 distinct seeded generators per condition; data generator uses ten unnamed channels, hidden five synchronized oscillator pairs, optionally slow common AR(1) center. Train ordinary centered covariance matching, measure Hilbert phase locking, evaluate chronological heldout center correlation and masked-channel prediction against common-only, partner-only and ordinary Ridge 9-input baseline; permute partner residual at holdout. Record exact CSV and all source code. A second phase persistence diagnostic was explicitly added AFTER first data review and labeled exploratory.

**Observed successes:** Full and pair-only both recovered 5/5 planted pairs 20/20 and PLV≈.89 vs no pair controls≈.10. Pair+common two-feature mask prediction MSE .286 vs 9-input Ridge .289; shuffling partner residual increases model MSE to 2.364. Learnable ordinary temporal source proxies are feasible.

**Observed failures/limits:** Primary center oracle threshold R²>.5 FAILS in FULL (mean .441, only 9/20 runs satisfy); in center-only .613, indicating source modes can mask naive hub estimator. Ordinary ridge is competitive; no new SMFT-specific prediction advantage. Planted five-source modes and shared center are provided by artificial data. Heldout mask manipulation is *predictor feature perturbation*, not intervention on real generative source. No LuoShu or natural trained Transformer.

**Formal countermodel:** `X_t=S_t`, `Y_t=X_{t-1}` and `X_t=S_t`, `Y_t=S_{t-1}` have identical observational law but different responses to do-intervention on X. A stable phase lead does not prove directed causal flow or an Observer operator. Separate **correlation, phase coherence, orientation, causal direction, and source center** as typed observables.

**Failure mode:** LLM sees phase-lock and 100% pair matching, retrofits entire field-body or LuoShu theorem and overlooks FAILED center endpoint. It counts 256 time points as 256 independent trials or silently promotes follow-on exploratory analysis as registered primary outcome. It interprets predictor ablation as a true causal manipulation of generator.

**Reusable rule:** `TemporalSourceTypecheck`: preregister pair, center, orientation, phase, persistent relaxation independently; audit generator-implanted structure and observables; carry null and ordinary baseline through; mark center negative results even alongside positive pair finding; require separate latent intervention to infer causal direction. `EvidenceGradeGate`: differentiate bounded synthetic ordinary association, trained neural, real natural Transformer and independent cross-model replication.

**Generative residual:** Improve center estimation with spectral separation and train-before-intervention control, test whether learned source coupling forecasts unobserved trace across task/seed/OOD, choose invariant phase/direction measures and explicit causal experiments. Then attempt stage-B LuoShu projection blinded to target pattern. Benchmark against spectral PCA, VAR, Hopfield, correlation matching, ridge.
