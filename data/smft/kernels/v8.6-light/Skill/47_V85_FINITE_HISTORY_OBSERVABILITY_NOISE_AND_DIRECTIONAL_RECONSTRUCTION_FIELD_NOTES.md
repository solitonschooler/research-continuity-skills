**New failure gate:** Algebraic uniqueness is not stable inference. **Directional identifiability:** A signed externally imposed skew operator changes matched-state derivatives by 2ω Ax. A test must include an excitation Ax≠0 and an orientation-sensitive measurement. Uniform states hide the sign, and a reflection-symmetric state can give identical scalar energy under ±ω. A model-aware oracle recovers ω exactly; that is an algebraic identity, not evidence a model discovered LuoShu's clockwise or counterclockwise flight.
