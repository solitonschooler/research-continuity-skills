# 7. v0.5 Exact Port Qualification Benchmark — completed synthetic M4

**Purpose and provenance.** This section is the `COMPILER`'s NEW formal/simulation example, not a claim discovered in natural Transformer weights. It implements a **finite exact** known-circuit test of `(PORT-3)` from `SRC-0259` and `SRC-0261`; the theorem remains M3, exhaustive synthetic checks M4. Executable full script: `06_BENCHMARK_LOCAL_ONLY/benchmarks/port_toy.py` in the distributable ZIP. Independent complete rebuild from the equations below is possible without the script.

## 7.1 Declaration and operational partition

Let the internal A state be `s=(c,h)∈{0,1}²`, where `c` is incoming binary carry and `h` is nuisance; downstream B state `b∈ℤ` is an accumulated result; allowed input `u=(x,y,k)` has `x,y∈{0,1}` and position `k∈{0,1,2,3}`. This is a per-bit ripple-carry *known circuit*, not an attention model. Define

\[
\begin{aligned}
 t&=x\oplus y\oplus c, \\
 c'&=\mathbf1\{x+y+c\ge2\},\qquad h'=h\oplus x\oplus y,\\
 F((c,h),b,u)&=(c',h'),\\
 G((c,h),b,u)&=b+2^k t,\\
 H((c,h),b,u)&=t,\quad\Pi_c(c,h)=c.
\end{aligned}
\]

`F,G,H` are not arbitrary aliases: `F` advances A, `G` updates the downstream accumulator, `H` exposes the bit. For same-carry states, all three depend only on `c` and `u`, hence `(PORT-3)` holds for `Π_c`; the effective reduced functions are the displayed formulas with `h` deleted. The full B domain is ℤ; for THIS circuit, pairwise differences `G(s₁,b,u)−G(s₂,b,u)` cancel `b`, so testing `b=0,1` suffices for every integer b. This simplification is not legitimate for arbitrary systems. All successor A states remain valid and B is closed.

**Exact enumeration:** four A states, twelve controls, two sampled B representatives. For `Π_c`, there are two nonidentical same-port state pairs, giving `2×12×2=48` pair/control/B comparisons. `E_F,E_G,E_H` are the fraction of those comparisons violating their corresponding equality. For the other ports, denominators differ; thus do not compare the resulting error fractions as distribution-free effect sizes. This is an exhaustive statement only for the declared finite A/control domain.

## 7.2 Measured program outputs (2026-10-14, local Python)

| circuit / port | carry-probe decoding accuracy | E_F | E_G | E_H | ideal information bits | conclusion |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Correct circuit / `Π(c,h)=c` | 100% | 0 | 0 | 0 | 1 | nontrivial qualified exact Port |
| Correct circuit / nuisance `Π=h` | 50% | 0 | 1 | 1 | 1 | fails joint model |
| Correct circuit / XOR `Π=c⊕h` | 50% | 0.5 | 1 | 1 | 1 | fails |
| Correct circuit / constant | 50% | 0 | 2/3 | 2/3 | 0 | overcompressed |
| Correct circuit / identity `Π=(c,h)` | 100% | — | — | — | 2 | mathematically sufficient but no nontrivial fiber pairs; VACUOUS test |
| **F-only fault** / carry | 100% | **1** | 0 | 0 | 1 | first condition independently necessary |
| **G-only fault** / carry | **100%** | 0 | **1** | 0 | 1 | **perfect visible task probe, invalid downstream joint Port** |
| **H-only fault** / carry | 100% | 0 | 0 | **1** | 1 | third condition independently necessary |

Faults are explicit counterfactual alterations: `F-only` sets `c' ← c' ⊕ h`, `G-only` adds `2h` to the accumulator, `H-only` sets visible `t ← t ⊕ h`. In particular `G-only` maintains an observationally perfect decoding of carry and a nuisance-independent immediate H while rendering the proposed 1-bit Port insufficient. This is the central falsification of "100% carry probe ⇒ functional sufficiency". Six Python `unittest` checks pass; all 256 pairs of 4-bit numbers satisfy the hand-coded carry arithmetic oracle.

**Checkpoint drift stress:** at checkpoint `base` the encoded state stores `(c,h)` and `Π=first` qualifies (`0,0,0`). At synthetic checkpoint `swapped` the encoding is `(h,c)` with the SAME underlying circuit. The stale `Π=first` fails `(0,1,1)`; `Π=second` requalifies `(0,0,0)`. This is a deliberately engineered basis permutation, not observed representational drift in learned neural checkpoints. Do not regard index choice as an invariant identity of a natural neural individual.

A purely sampled zero-failure observation is **not** a proof of exact sufficiency. For `n` independent Bernoulli comparisons with zero observed failures, an illustrative one-sided 95% binomial upper bound is `1−0.05^(1/n)` (about 7.22% for n=40); actual neural interventions violate independence assumptions unless sampling and clustering are specified. Exhaustive finite enumeration is stronger here only because the toy domain is fully declared.

# 10. v0.6 K2-AI-002: Multi-bit Hidden-State Port — executed bounded experiment

**Compiler addition**, 2026-10-14. Applies the conditional Port theorem of `SRC-0259` / `SRC-0261` to a **trained synthetic Transformer**. The source authors did not make the below measurements. Evidence: `M4` on this synthetic artificial arithmetic domain; **not** `M5` natural model evidence, no proof of emergence/attractor fusion, no observed octonionic geometry. Earlier v0.5 §7 circuit and §8 blind protocol remain valid. Full reproduction code/results is in local-only `06_BENCHMARK_LOCAL_ONLY` (not uploaded as Project items).

## 10.1 Declare the complete experiment; why it differs from v0.5

We construct a 1-layer, 4-head, d=32 `TransformerEncoderLayer` with CLS token, plus a **separate** MLP decoder consuming (i) the learned **post-encoder CLS hidden state** `h ∈ R^32` and (ii) the current digit pair `u=(x,y)∈{0,1}²`. Prefixes are LSB-first: `p=((x_0,y_0),…,(x_{n−1},y_{n−1}))`. Define oracle incoming carry recursively from `c_0=0`, `c_{j+1}=floor((x_j+y_j+c_j)/2)`. The model must predict next sum bit and next carry from `h(p)` and `u`:

\[
\begin{aligned}
 t(c,u)&=(x+y+c)\bmod 2,\\
 c'(c,u)&=\lfloor(x+y+c)/2\rfloor,\\
 z(h,u)&=\mathrm{MLP}([h;\mathrm{Embed}(u)]),\\
 H(h,u)&=\operatorname{argmax}(\mathrm{Head}_{sum}(z)),\\
 F_{bit}(h,u)&=\operatorname{argmax}(\mathrm{Head}_{carry}(z)).
\end{aligned}
\]

Input pairs are tokens 0–3, CLS token 4, PAD token 5; sinusoidal positions; padding masks. Train on **all** prefix contexts at n=1,2 and **52/64** contexts at n=3, crossed with all 4 current-digit pairs (`72 × 4 = 288` training examples). Twelve held-out n=3 prefixes yield 48 held-out examples. Exhaustively evaluate 256 n=4 prefix contexts × 4 current inputs (1,024) and 1,024 n=5 prefixes × 4 (4,096): these lengths are entirely **out of training distribution**. Fixed split seed 101; model seeds 7 and 17; full-batch AdamW, 80 steps, learning rate 0.007; PyTorch CPU 2.10.0. We report 0,20,40,80 checkpoint task accuracies in JSON. This is a small teacher-forced **prefix/one-bit transition** model, NOT an end-to-end autoregressive multi-bit adder.

`A` state = the Transformer CLS hidden `h(p)`. Candidate minimal *behavioral* Port label on the finite protocol = carry of prefix, `\Pi_c(h(p)) := c(p)` **only if representable as a function of h**; linear probe is separately trained/evaluated as an empirical identifiability check. **Dynamic-state caveat (non-negotiable):** this specific network has no learned recurrent transition `h_{t+1}=F_A(h_t,u)`; it predicts a *next carry bit*. Therefore `F_bit` is **not** directly a measured `\Pi(F_A(h,u))`; our `E_F` is a **next-carry output surrogate**, not a complete test of PORT-3’s projected neural state evolution. The full dynamic functional-interface theorem remains untested on this architecture.

The intervention replaces **the entire internal CLS hidden vector** with that of a valid donor prefix while holding the receiver's `u` fixed. The donor is drawn from a syntactically valid prefix context. For n=4/5, donors are valid **grammar states**, but *outside training support*: never call them training-distribution on-manifold. We do not yet perform a one-bit causal do-intervention by rewriting the physical hidden state to enforce a selected carry value.

Two operational B declarations must be kept separate:

1. `B_out` = sum accumulator. `G_out(b,h,u)=b+2^k H(h,u)`. In this narrow task, **E_G_out is identically E_H**, therefore these two residual axes are NOT independent. Passing them cannot establish full PORT-3 for a larger downstream system.
2. `B_z` = the actual decoder intermediate vector `z(h,u) ∈ R^32`, if it is accessible/consumed as next-stage state. `G_z(b,h,u):=z(h,u)`. Then its equality under same-Port fiber pairs is an **additional** demanding requirement. A change in `z` need not matter if no downstream consumer uses it; the protocol must specify whether it matters.

## 10.2 Fiber tests — exact for discrete F/H, sampled for continuous G_z

For fixed prefix length n, compare ALL unordered pairs of distinct prefixes satisfying **oracle** `c(p_1)=c(p_2)`, each at all four current pairs `u`. Exhaustive (in this declared finite subset) discrete residuals:

\[
 E_F^{disc}=\frac{\#\{(p_1,p_2,u):F_{bit}(h(p_1),u)\ne F_{bit}(h(p_2),u)\}}{\#\{(p_1,p_2,u)\}}, \quad
 E_H^{disc}=\frac{\#\{(p_1,p_2,u):H(h(p_1),u)\ne H(h(p_2),u)\}}{\#\{(p_1,p_2,u)\}}.
\]

Compute exact counts by class histograms rather than stochastic sampling. This is an exhaustive comparison of **finite input contexts for a fixed trained network**, not a universal theorem that the latent carry abstraction is globally closed. The **G_z** stress uses 500 seeded pairs per length/seed within same-oracle-carry fibers; report share with `||z_1−z_2||_2 > 0.5`. **0.5 is an illustrative engineering threshold; it was not independently calibrated before seeing results.** Sensitivity at 0.1, 0.5 and 2.0 is retained in JSON. A sampled failure is a witness that `z` is not invariant at the specified tolerance; sampled zero is never proof of invariance.

|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 7 | held-out length 3 | 100.00% | 100.00% | 120 | 0.00% | 0.00% | 28.60% |

**Core inference:** on n=4, seed 7 has `E_F=E_H=0` across all 65,280 declared same-carry pair/input comparisons and 100% task/probe accuracy. **Yet** 42% of sampled same-carry pairs have decoder-state L2 distance greater than 0.5. It is thus **behaviorally congruent for the emitted carry/sum channels under B_out on this finite task**, but **not qualified under B_z at tolerance 0.5**, and no learned next-hidden-state closure has been measured. Seed 17 fails behavioral closure already at n=4. At n=5 both seeds fail with nonzero E_F/E_H. This is the main v0.6 research result: closure is *protocol and horizon dependent*; “the model learned carry” does not imply a stable unrestricted reusable Port.

Controls: for each seed/length, additionally sample 500 pairs with opposite oracle carry (`cross`), arbitrary pairs grouped by constant Port, and pairs matched only on the final prefix digit (`last_digit_null`). These controls mostly yield different outputs under equal-current-u and expose why a suitable equivalence relation matters; see JSON for exact figures. The n=4 seed-7 null `last_digit` has sampled `E_F ≈ 12.2%`, `E_H ≈ 24.6%`, while `same-carry` has 0/0. A trivial full identity Port has no distinct fiber pairs and cannot be credited as effective compression.

## 10.3 Reconstructability and *operationally typed* non-implications

- A **perfect linear probe** on finitely many hidden vectors implies finite-sample decodability, not causal necessity, sufficiency over an open hidden-state neighborhood, or latent algebraic closure. The reported fiber grouping uses **oracle incoming carry**, not the probe's predictions. On n=5, observed probe errors explicitly prevent a globally valid reconstructed Port from being claimed.
- Interchanging an entire trained hidden vector from a donor context only tests downstream behavior under a *naturally produced valid vector*. It does NOT isolate a one-bit carry causal channel, disentangle nuisance factors, or prove a unique naturally individuated attractor. `cross` differences demonstrate sensitivity, but that could be via many correlated latent features.
- If `B_z` is a downstream state, full approximate PORT-3 requires an epsilon statement for its update, not just equality of task outputs. Whether preserving `z` is truly required depends on its actual reuse/counterfactual context, which this experiment does not test.
- We measure **no actual compute-speed, memory or deployment benefit** from a 1-bit Port. A label needing a separately trained linear probe is not a free compressed internal replacement. We have not trained a downstream module to operate on the 1-bit extracted carry instead of h.
- Two model seeds are limited replications, not a reliable population confidence interval. Pair outcomes share prefix contexts and are not iid Bernoulli trials. Conditional exact finite counts do not establish behavior on all possible neural hidden vectors.
- K2 math remains conditional: a genuine Port demands well-typed A/B dynamics, empirically justified equivalence classes and counterfactual closure on the intended intervention set. There is no evidence here of `G_2/SO(4)` in neural activations or Five-Regime uniqueness.
