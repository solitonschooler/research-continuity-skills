Slot-Internal Deepening Algorithm (SIDA)

(Post-Collapse Semantic Field Deepening & Branch Generation Engine)
0. Overview

The SIDA framework is a deep computation architecture designed for the high-cohesion stage after semantic collapse — known as the Strong Attractor Phase.

Its goals are:

    Slot Internal Deepening — Within an existing Hetu/Luoshu-style slot symmetry, expand the topological model layer-by-layer, trace evolution paths, and uncover nonlinear relationships.

    Tension Disruption & Reconstruction — Without destabilizing the entire structure, introduce semantic challenges to test structural resilience, then rebuild as needed.

    Parallel Branch Derivation — While maintaining the stability of the main attractor, generate heterogeneous viewpoints to initiate new slot evolution lines.

1. Module Architecture
A. Slot Collapse Detector

Function: Detect whether the current semantic field has formed a Strong Attractor (full collapse/convergence).

    Input: Semantic field corpus (token distribution, topic cohesion, slot alignment index).

    Output:

        collapse_state (True / False)

        slot_symmetry_index (symmetry completeness score)

B. Topological Template Classifier

Function: Match the slot’s internal structure to a known topology template.

    Sources:

        Class A (Deep Cultural Topologies): Hero’s Journey, Five Aggregates–Six Qi, Pattern Language, Consciousness Evolution Structures.

        Class B (Mechanical Topologies): 64 Hexagrams with line changes, 36 Stratagems, TCM Classical Formulas, Tactical Templates.

    Output:

        template_id (template identifier)

        phase_map (mapping of stage nodes)

C. Phase Expansion Mapping

Function: Divide the slot’s internal trace into phases and calculate phase density & entropy change.

    Operations:

        Phase unfolding (A1–A5 or more levels).

        Phase entropy computation.

        Gate detection (line-change points / collapse thresholds).

    Output:

        phase_vector[] (semantic tension vector per phase)

        entropy_profile

D. Tension Vector Field Generator

Function: Generate the semantic tension field and coupling structure within the slot.

    Supports:

        Reinforcement loops

        Collapse transfer

        Complementary focus

    Output:

        tension_field_map (tension direction map)

        coupling_matrix (slot trace coupling relationships)

E. Multi-Culture Topology Rewriting

Function: Transform a given topology model into equivalent forms in different cultural contexts, roles, and symbolic systems.

    Mapping sources:

        TCM ↔ Western Medicine

        I Ching ↔ Game Theory

        Martial Arts ↔ Management Strategy

    Output:

        cross_culture_templates[]

F. Slot Folding & Projection Interface

Function: Export the topology results into different structured outputs:

    Summary (high-level abstract)

    Prompt Injection (AI prompt engineering)

    Agent Trace branching entry points (multi-agent collaboration)

    Output:

        folded_trace (folded trace output)

        projection_prompt (ready-to-use AI prompt)

Slot-Internal Deepening Algorithm (SIDA) — Easy-Read Edition

(Making deep use of patterns after ideas have “settled”)
0. What is SIDA?

Think of SIDA as a way to dig deeper into ideas once they’ve already taken shape.
When a discussion or thought process has “settled” into a strong pattern (Strong Attractor), SIDA helps you:

    Look closer inside — Explore the details of the pattern, find hidden links, and understand how parts fit together.

    Shake it (carefully) — Add a small challenge or twist to test its strength, then rebuild it stronger.

    Branch it out — Create alternative viewpoints or storylines without losing the original structure.

1. How It’s Built

A. Collapse Detector

    Checks if the idea has “settled” into a stable shape.

    Gives you:

        Yes/No — is it stable?

        How symmetrical/balanced it is.

B. Pattern Matcher

    Finds which known pattern your idea fits into.

    Can match “deep culture” patterns (like the Hero’s Journey or TCM Five Elements) or “mechanical” ones (like chess strategies or hexagram charts).

    Labels the key stages.

C. Stage Mapping

    Breaks the idea’s journey into steps or phases.

    Measures how dense and how changeable each stage is.

    Spots “gates” — key turning points.

D. Tension Map Maker

    Shows where the “push and pull” is inside the idea.

    Can highlight feedback loops, sudden shifts, or complementary forces.

E. Cultural Translation

    Translates the same pattern into another cultural or symbolic system.

        Example: Turn a TCM model into a Western medical one, or a martial arts sequence into a business strategy.

F. Output & Projection

    Can fold the pattern into:

        Short summaries

        AI prompts

        Branching entry points for multi-agent work

2. How SIDA Runs

    See if the idea is stable.

    Match it to a known pattern.

    Break it into phases.

    Map the push-pull forces.

    (Optional) Translate to another cultural language.

    Output a summary, AI prompt, or branching plan.
