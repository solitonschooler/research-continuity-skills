# Plain-English quick-start prompts

You do not need to know the JSON Schema to begin. These examples are suggested user requests, not executable commands or mandatory workflow templates.

**Quick scan (bounded):**

> Use Research Kernel Builder on this folder of 12 research notes. Identify the most consequential source-backed results and contradictions. One round only. Do not run experiments. Tell me what you actually read, what is unresolved, and how to resume.

**Verify and improve selected claims:**

> Use Research Kernel Builder. Consolidate the sources, then choose up to two high-impact claims for mathematical challenge and conditional repair. Preserve the exact original assertions and distinguish newly invented derivations. Stop after two rounds.

**Historical AI coding-agent audit:**

> Use Research Archaeologist on this design history and AI agent log. Determine whether the reported fix was exercised by tests and whether a later change invalidated it. Separate documented evidence from conjectured causes. Provide an improvement receipt and a resumable checkpoint.

**Continue an interrupted project:**

> Read this prior research-state JSON and its cited available sources. Verify the source identities and unresolved issues before changing anything. Continue only the highest-value pending research question; do not silently overwrite earlier claims.

**Stop:**

> STOP now. Provide the best currently supportable result, what was and was not checked, files actually saved, unresolved items, and a copyable restart point. Do not claim a durable checkpoint unless one exists.

**Optional deep experiment:**

> Propose a discriminating numerical test with ordinary baselines and negative controls. Do not execute it unless you ask first and I approve the tools, costs and data use.
