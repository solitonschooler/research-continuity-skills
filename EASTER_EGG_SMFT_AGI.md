# Easter Egg: A Cross-Layer Toy Model of AGI Is Hiding in This Repository

> **You came for Research Continuity Skills. There is also a research program hiding in the example data.**
>
> The included **SMFT (Semantic Meme Field Theory) / World Formation** Kernel does not merely illustrate how to organize hundreds of papers. It preserves an evolving attempt to model how locally interacting processes might become organized, acquire task-relevant boundaries, sustain reusable functions, and eventually participate in a self-revising agent.
>
> **This is an exploratory engineering *toy architecture*, not an implemented AGI, an established model of how Transformers think, or a proof of spontaneous semantic attractors.** Its value is that many of the proposed interfaces, counterexamples, and conditional mathematical results are concrete enough to challenge or test.

## Why is this here?

The two Skills in this repository grew out of roughly 86 iterations of AI-assisted consolidation, mathematical checking, error correction, and research development across **325 original research documents**. The original collection concerns SMFT, *Fractal Organization* (《皇極經濟》), P8D, PORE, SIDA, world formation, AI, economics, mathematics, and related subjects.

The resulting sample Kernel is unusually revealing: you can follow *both* the proposed theory and the ways it was narrowed, repaired, or contradicted by later investigation. The Research Skills are independent of SMFT's scientific correctness. But the sample is also an invitation to examine the theory itself.

## The surprise: a layered engineering blueprint

A reader can reconstruct the following **candidate** architecture from the Kernel. This diagram is a synthesis for orientation, **not** a claim that the complete pipeline has been built or that every arrow is mathematically established.

```text
Signals, interactions, perturbations, learning
                   |
        [L0] Micro-dynamics
        x(t+1) = F(x(t), u(t))
        candidate collective structure / attractors
                   |
        [L1] Observer and world declaration
        boundary B; measurement protocol P
        effective state z = Pi(x)
                   |
        [L2] P8D typed functional interfaces
        potential / throughput | boundary / exchange
        trigger / propagation | path / admissibility
                   |
        [L3] Qualified functional ports
        jointly preserve relevant outputs AND
        downstream updates under permitted interventions
                   |
        [L4] Adaptive agent governance
        Generate -> Activate -> Close -> Select -> Retain
        guarded mode changes; revisable declarations
                   |
        [L5] Research / decision memory
        accepted trace L+ | unresolved residual L-
        evidence, challenge, repair, re-declaration
                   |
                   +------ feedback to earlier layers
```

The interesting proposal is not that these six layers are always found in nature. It is that they provide **separable engineering obligations** for studying possible self-organization and for building agents that learn, act, check, remember, and revise.

### L0 — From turbulence to candidate organization

Begin with a specified dynamical or computational system, for example a recurrent network, interacting software agents, or a trainable model. Investigate whether repeated interactions produce metastable patterns, basins, coordinated transitions, or attracting sets.

A mathematical *attractor* requires a defined state space, update law, and notion of attraction. A persistent pattern is not automatically an attractor; an attractor is not automatically semantic; and neither proves intelligence. The origin of a genuinely task-relevant, naturally emerging semantic attractor in a trained Transformer remains **open** in this research program.

This is a **conditional quotient criterion**, not a proof that the chosen effective state is an objectively unique component of reality. The boundary can be revised when neglected information becomes important.

### L1 — A Bounded Observer Declares What Counts as a World

The Kernel distinguishes the underlying process from what a bounded observer can measure and act upon.

An observer frame is schematically defined as:

$$
\boxed{\Gamma=(B,\Pi),\qquad \Pi:X_B\to Z}
$$

where:

- $B$ selects an operational boundary.
- $\Pi$ retains distinctions relevant to a declared task.

For every admissible action $u$, an exact effective dynamics must satisfy the **transition-preservation condition**:

$$
\boxed{\Pi\bigl(T_u(x)\bigr)=S_u\bigl(\Pi(x)\bigr)}
$$

### L2 — P8D: eight names, four different kinds of engineering problems

P8D II uses four **typed complementary pairs**, inspired by the trigrams of the *I Ching*, as a proposed functional grammar—not as eight interchangeable coordinates or a universal law:

| Pair                | Engineering question                                                         |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Qian–Kun** | What potential difference can become *qualified throughput*?               |
| **Gen–Dui**  | What boundary must persist, and what exchange should cross it?               |
| **Zhen–Xun** | What triggers action, and how does activation propagate?                     |
| **Kan–Li**   | Which candidate paths can be realized, and which transitions are admissible? |

In particular, **Kan** is not simply “memory” and **Li** is not simply “focus.” The more precise distinction is *path realization* versus *transition admissibility*. These typed roles might inspire a modular agent design; their number and cultural names are not established necessities for AGI.

### L3 — A functional port must survive causal testing

Suppose a large hidden state \(x\) is compressed into a candidate functional port \(p=\Pi_{port}(x)\). Decoding the right answer from \(p\) is not enough. Two hidden states with the same \(p\) must also produce appropriately equivalent downstream behavior under allowed interventions if the port is claimed to be an exact sufficient interface.

This creates a decisive distinction:

- **Engineered functional port:** a deliberately constructed abstraction, tested for the stated task and intervention set.
- **Naturally formed functional individual:** a persistent, causally organized structure shown to arise in an actual trained model without being inserted by the investigator.

The first can be studied with ordinary state-space mathematics and causal experiments. **The second has not been demonstrated by the Kernel's conditional theory or synthetic tests.**

### L4 — A five-regime controller can govern ongoing development

One operational branch proposes a hybrid controller with five possible **duties**, not five mandatory sequential cognitive stages:

| Mode                      | Duty                                      | Critical safeguard                               |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| **G — Generation** | Produce viable alternatives               | Avoid uncontrolled idea explosion                |
| **A — Activation** | Act or simulate to reveal effects         | Distinguish action from evidence                 |
| **C — Closure**    | Form a provisional coherent commitment    | Do not commit before admissibility checks        |
| **S — Selection**  | Compare, falsify, prune                   | Include negative controls and standard baselines |
| **R — Retention**  | Preserve lessons and unresolved anomalies | Do not erase failed ideas or their causes        |

The controller should have guarded transitions, independent rules for changing its **declaration** and **purpose**, and persistent records across resets. Whether five modes outperform four, six, or an ordinary controller is an **open model-selection problem**.

### L5 — A ledger that permits the agent to change its mind

The framework distinguishes accepted, source-scoped history \(L^+\) from unresolved residuals and conflicting evidence \(L^-\). A new agent should be able to reconstruct *why* a conclusion was accepted, *what would invalidate it*, and *which questions remain open*. A residual is a candidate diagnostic, **not** automatic proof of hidden structure.

Here the SMFT toy architecture meets this GitHub repository's two practical Skills: **Research Kernel Builder** preserves and updates the research state; **Research Archaeologist** challenges how that state was formed and what it may have missed.

## Two Optional Mathematical Side Doors

### The Geometry Side Door

Another branch investigates the typed chain:

$$
\boxed{
\mathbb{O}
\;\longrightarrow\;
G_2/\mathrm{SO}(4)
\;\longrightarrow\;
\mathbb{H}
\;\longrightarrow\;
\mathbb{C}^{2}
}
$$

as a candidate way to select a quaternionic subalgebra and construct lower-dimensional effective structure. Here \(\mathbb O\) means the **octonions**, not the bounded observer. This is an algebraic/geometric research construction; no established link makes it the representation learned by a Transformer.

**The HeTu–LuoShu side door.** Later investigations test assumed **center-plus-five-pair** source graphs, projected observations, directional dynamics, and persistent circulation. In v8.6, undriven transverse modes decay; externally driven stochastic models can sustain *probability flux*; and an actively driven nonlinear model can have a stable deterministic cycle. The center-feedback ablation shows that the center is **not necessary** for that last synthetic cycle. None of these results demonstrates spontaneous HeTu formation, numerical LuoShu 1–9 placement, a natural nine-period Flying-Star sequence, or emergence in a real pretrained Transformer.

This mix of attractive ideas **and disconfirming tests** is part of what makes the archive interesting.

## A small experiment you can ask your agent to design

Use a toy model that learns **binary addition with carry**, and treat a candidate carry representation as a functional port:

1. Define an observable task, held-out carry cases, state boundaries, and allowed interventions.
2. Find a compact candidate port and compare it with an ordinary task-matched baseline.
3. Check whether states with equivalent port values have equivalent relevant downstream effects, not just similar decodability.
4. Perturb or ablate the port, track what breaks, and repeat across training checkpoints and seeds.
5. Compare simple fixed controllers against optional G/A/C/S/R governance, at equal computation and intervention budgets.
6. Preserve failures, negative controls, counterexamples, and uncertainty as first-class results.

This is a **proposed test protocol**, not a claimed experiment already completed on a real Transformer by the v8.6 project. If it fails, the failure itself may improve the architecture.

## A challenge, not a sales pitch

Try asking a fresh LLM:

> **Read the SMFT v8.6 Light Kernel. Reconstruct its most defensible cross-layer toy architecture for an adaptive AI agent. Separate source claims, conditional mathematics, synthetic evidence, analogies, and untested emergence hypotheses. Identify the three weakest interfaces and propose experiments that could genuinely falsify them. Do not treat an attractive metaphor as a neural mechanism.**

If the model comes back with sharper definitions, a smaller viable architecture, or a counterexample that breaks a cherished assumption, it has understood something important about the project.

**The Easter egg is not that AGI has been solved. It is that a real, unusually sprawling attempt to think about its organization is sitting here—together with the evidence needed to disagree with it.**
